#1 Priority Field · Learn First

Excel & Business Data Analytics

Definition: Spreadsheet software such as Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets organizes, calculates, and analyzes data. Business data analytics is the process of examining business information to find patterns, evaluate performance, and make informed decisions.

Why entrepreneurs need it

Sales numbers become useful when a business can answer questions such as:

  • How much revenue did we earn?
  • Which product brings in the highest sales?
  • Are we profitable after costs?
  • Which month had the strongest performance?
  • What should we stock or promote next?
Sample situation: A milk tea shop records daily orders. Excel calculates the total sales, while a chart reveals that weekends consistently outperform weekdays.

  • Accept and manage orders online.

Field 04

Artificial Intelligence for Business

Definition: Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, generating text, making predictions, and assisting with decisions.

Business applications

  • Draft product descriptions and marketing messages
  • Summarize customer feedback
  • Help answer frequently asked questions
  • Predict demand using historical data
  • Brainstorm product names and business ideas
Important: AI-generated content can be inaccurate. Verify prices, facts, legal claims, and customer-facing information. Do not paste private customer information into unapproved AI tools.

Field 05

Business Information Systems

Definition: Business information systems combine people, procedures, data, and technology to support everyday business operations and management decisions.

Types and purposes

  • POS (Point of Sale): Records customer purchases
  • Inventory system: Tracks stock levels
  • Accounting system: Organizes financial transactions
  • ERP: Connects business processes across departments
  • CRM: Manages customer interactions
Example: A grocery’s POS records every checkout and can update stock levels in an integrated inventory system.

Field 06

Cybersecurity & Data Privacy

Definition: Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, accounts, and data against unauthorized access, theft, disruption, and other digital threats. Data privacy concerns the responsible handling of personal information.

Why it matters for entrepreneurs

Even a small business may handle passwords, payment information, supplier documents, customer records, and social media accounts.

  • Use strong unique passwords and multi-factor authentication
  • Watch for fake payment confirmations and phishing
  • Keep software updated and back up business data
  • Limit employee access to only what they need
  • Collect and protect personal information responsibly

Field 07

Business Process Automation

Definition: Business process automation uses software and digital rules to handle repetitive activities with less manual effort.

Purpose & examples

  • Send order confirmations automatically
  • Update inventory after recorded purchases
  • Generate weekly sales reports
  • Notify staff when stock falls below a threshold
  • Route customer inquiries to the right team
Example: A small bakery could trigger a reorder alert when the system detects fewer than 10 packs of flour.

Website Development & No-Code Tools

Definition: Website development involves creating and maintaining web pages and web applications. No-code tools allow people to build simple websites, forms, and workflows through visual interfaces without writing traditional program code.

Purpose & examples

  • Display services, hours, pricing, and contact details

  • Publish menus, announcements, and booking information

Example tools include WordPress, Wix, Google Sites, and online form builders; capabilities vary by platform.

Field 09

Databases & Customer Relationship Management

Definition: A database is an organized collection of information that can be stored, searched, updated, and managed. Customer relationship management (CRM) refers to processes and systems for tracking and improving interactions with customers.

Purpose & business examples

  • Maintain reliable product and inventory records
  • Track orders and purchase histories
  • Search customer inquiries efficiently
  • Coordinate follow-ups and after-sales support
  • Identify returning customers and loyalty opportunities
Example: A repair shop maintains a customer database containing job numbers, service dates, devices, and repair statuses.

Field 10

Digital Payments & Financial Technology

Definition: Digital payments transfer money electronically rather than exchanging physical cash. Financial technology (fintech) refers to technology used to deliver and improve financial services.

Purpose & business uses

  • Accept e-wallet, bank transfer, card, and QR-based payments
  • Offer customers more payment choices
  • Record electronic payment references and reconcile sales
  • Support online checkout
  • Monitor payment fees, settlements, and refunds
Example: A food kiosk accepts a supported QR payment and checks confirmation from its actual merchant account before releasing an order.
